# EZ Zoom — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 21, 2026_

EZ Zoom is a browser extension that lets you zoom in on web pages using your mouse pointer, keyboard shortcuts, and a hold-key gesture.

## Data collection

**EZ Zoom does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any user data. Period.**

- No personal information is collected.
- No browsing history, page content, or keystrokes are recorded or transmitted.
- No analytics, telemetry, or tracking of any kind is included.
- The extension makes no network requests to any server.

## Local storage

Your settings (zoom amount, maximum zoom, animation speed, hold-key choice, hover zoom level, and toggles) are stored using Chrome's built-in `chrome.storage.sync` API. This data stays within your own browser and Google account sync (if you have Chrome sync enabled) and is never accessible to the developer.

## Permissions explained

- **storage** — used solely to save your zoom preferences.
- **Access to web pages (content script)** — required to apply the visual zoom transform to the page you are viewing and to read the mouse position and modifier-key state for zoom gestures. Nothing is stored or transmitted.
