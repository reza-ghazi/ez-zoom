Orbital resonances in the outer belt

Small bodies trapped in mean-motion resonances with a giant planet keep a stable phase relation over millions of orbits. The 3:2 resonance protects objects whose orbits cross the planet's path, because conjunctions always happen far from the crossing point.

Survey data show a clear concentration of objects near the resonance centre, with a libration amplitude that shrinks as eccentricity grows. The table and diagram summarise the measured populations.
